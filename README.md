# 🐾 FocusPet - Your Interactive Desktop Companion

**FocusPet** is a smart, context-aware desktop companion designed to boost your productivity while keeping you entertained. It’s not just a static pet; it reacts to your digital behavior in real-time!

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Mood Awareness (Context Sensing):**
    * 😠 **Angry Mode:** If you get distracted by social media (Facebook, Instagram, etc.), HAZA Pet gets grumpy to remind you to get back to work!
    * 😊 **Friendly Mode:** When you are coding or learning (VS Code, GitHub, etc.), HAZA Pet stays happy and supports your progress.
* **Interactive Touch:** Give it some love! Click or hover on the pet to interact and see its cute reactions.
* **Customizable Dashboard:** A dedicated settings panel to tweak the pet’s behavior, appearance, and distraction triggers.
* **Productivity Focused:** A fun way to stay away from distractions and stay focused on your goals.

---

## 🚀 How it Works

The pet monitors your active window titles to determine your current activity and switches its emotional state accordingly.

1.  **Focus Mode:** Triggered by coding tools and educational sites.
2.  **Distraction Mode:** Triggered by social media and entertainment platforms.
